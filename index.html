Description

Focused on teaching new and expecting parents everything they need to know about infant/toddler CPR and first aid.

This 2.5-hour class includes hands-on practice, real-life scenario discussions, and interactive demonstrations. We practice CPR and choking techniques, discuss basic first aid, and walk through how to handle different emergencies with confidence.

You will leave feeling more prepared, with practical skills you hope you never have to use.

Class Details

  • Class Length: 2.5 hours
  • Best For: Expecting parents and new parents
  • Format: Interactive and hands-on

When should I take this class?

The ideal time is during pregnancy or in the first months after your baby arrives. Taking it early gives you time to learn, practice, and feel ready before emergencies ever happen.

Who should take it?

  • Expecting parents
  • New parents
  • Partners and primary caregivers
  • Anyone caring for infants and toddlers
